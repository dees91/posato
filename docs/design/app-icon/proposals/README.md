# Application icon proposals

Status: proposal B — Forest and the licenses navigation below accepted by the
maintainer on 2026-09-14. Final sources and exports are documented in
[the artwork reference](../README.md).

[Compare both directions](comparison.svg).

- **A — Paper:** warm surface with one-color moss mark, closest to the in-app wordmark.
- **B — Forest:** moss surface with sage and warm-white forms, borrowing the two-tone interval artwork. Recommended for stronger home-screen recognition.

Both preserve the two offset rounded forms and eight-degree rotation of
`PosatoMark`. Dark and monochrome tinted sources are included for comparison.
The board previews rounded platform masks; the square SVGs are source artwork,
not final iOS or macOS exports. No generated bitmap illustration is used.

## Information and licenses navigation

The final maintainer direction is **About Posato** beside the wordmark on
iPhone and below **On this Mac** in the Mac sidebar. That secondary screen
shows short app information, the installed version, and a **Licenses**
disclosure. The three legal documents remain readable in full with return
navigation. The accepted contract is recorded in the root `DESIGN.md`.
