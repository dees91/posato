# Intel Ventura helper registration recovery

- **Record path:** Recorded task
- **Review tier:** High-risk
- **Outcome:** Determine whether the notarized Intel Ventura candidate can complete helper setup after a controlled repair of the Mac's Background Task Management registration.
- **Boundary:** Only the explicitly authorized Intel test Mac. Preserve Posato application data and the installed bundle. Do not change proxy settings or weaken daemon authentication. A system-wide Background Items reset requires a fresh maintainer decision because it also changes other applications' approvals.
- **Dependencies:** The notarized Intel candidate and the launch correction in the Intel Ventura worktree; ADR 0004's cleanup and registration rules; the earlier MACOS-007 recovery evidence.
- **Acceptance:** Preserve ignored diagnostic evidence and verify no active Posato proxy ownership or enabled HTTP/HTTPS proxy before a separately authorized Mac restart after the recorded daemon approval. Identify all logged-in users, confirm their work is saved, and state that the restart ends GUI sessions and SSH. After reconnecting, read BTM, `launchd`, and proxy state before any in-app setup. If that fails, inventory every third-party Background Item and its prior approval state; verify no active Posato session, helper, daemon, lease, ownership record, standing grant, or enabled HTTP/HTTPS proxy before any registration reset. If the maintainer then authorizes a reset after seeing the concrete impact and restoration plan, use the documented system tool, restart, restore unrelated background-item preferences, register the existing signed Posato daemon, approve it through macOS, and prove `launchd` readiness and a completed in-app setup. If any reset precondition fails, stop without resetting.
- **Verification:** Record the tested revision, Ventura version, initial BTM and proxy state, exact commands, the daemon's resulting `SMAppService` and `launchd` state, and the application's setup result under ignored `build/verification/`. Do not claim Intel support from a successful launch alone.
