Posato 1.4

Longer quick choices: start a pause for 1, 2, 4, or 8 hours, or until the end of the day, next to 25 and 45 minutes.

Updates without the quit question: from 1.4, an update installed from Posato replaces it without asking "Quit Posato?". Updating from 1.2 or 1.3 to 1.4.0 still asks once while a schedule is on; choose Quit to continue. During a pause, Posato does not install the update; install it after the pause ends.

A calmer design: buttons and rows dim when you press them instead of showing a ripple.

Fixes: Posato no longer reports setup as incomplete when a busy Mac slows its background helper for a few minutes; one press of Remove workspace removes a workspace with a long iCloud history, and Posato shows that the removal is in progress.
