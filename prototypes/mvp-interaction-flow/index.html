Disposable interaction prototype

Can the Posato MVP flow stay clear when state becomes awkward?

This prototype tests whether onboarding, local mappings, a manual session, truthful synchronization, recovery, and intentional early termination form one understandable interaction model across a Mac and an iPhone.
